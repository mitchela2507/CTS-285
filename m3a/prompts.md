# AI Prompt Log — Fall Break Adventure

This document logs the staged prompts used during the planning, designing, and testing of *The Lost Artifact of Blackwood Manor*.

## Stage: idea

**Prompt:**

> Here are the sides for the assignment, and the zip with all necessary materials. I would like you to perform the interviewer role with ME as the customer, and the design and then build the game.

**What the AI gave me (one line):**
Gemini misunderstood who was supposed to be the customer at first, but after I corrected it, it began interviewing me about my game idea.

**What I kept, changed, or threw away:**
I corrected Gemini so that I could answer the interview questions myself. I used my answers to choose a mystery-and-adventure game.

## Stage: idea

**Prompt:**

> I like mystery and adventure stories where the character has to solve problems, explore places, and make choices that affect what happens next. I like stories with surprises and more than one possible ending.

**What the AI gave me (one line):**
Gemini acknowledged my story preferences and continued with the interview.

**What I kept, changed, or threw away:**
I kept the mystery-and-adventure theme with exploration, problem solving, meaningful choices, surprises, and different endings.

## Stage: idea

**Prompt:**

> I would like the game to take about 5–10 minutes to complete. I want enough choices to make the story interesting, but I don't want it to be so long that it becomes boring.

**What the AI gave me (one line):**
Gemini used my answer to understand how long I wanted the game to take.

**What I kept, changed, or threw away:**
I kept my goal of making the game interesting without making it too long or boring.

## Stage: idea

**Prompt:**

> I don't like when the choices don't make a difference, when the story repeats too much, or when I get stuck without knowing what to do next. I also don't want the descriptions to be too long.

**What the AI gave me (one line):**
Gemini summarized my preferences for meaningful choices, clear directions, and shorter descriptions.

**What I kept, changed, or threw away:**
I kept these preferences as guidelines for the game so that the choices matter and the player can follow the story without getting stuck.

## Stage: idea

**Prompt:**

> I want the choices I make to affect how the story ends. I would like at least two different endings, such as one where I successfully solve the mystery and another where I discover something unexpected. I also want the option to play again and make different choices.

**What the AI gave me (one line):**
Gemini identified different endings and replaying the game as things I wanted in my story.

**What I kept, changed, or threw away:**
I kept the idea of having different endings based on the player's choices and allowing the player to play again.

## Stage: idea

**What I kept, changed, or threw away:**
