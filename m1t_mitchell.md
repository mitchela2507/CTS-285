## Assumptions
- I have the loaf of bread with the peanut butter jar. I also have a knife to spread the peanut butter onto the bread and a plate to place the finished sandwich one. 

## Steps
- Take out 2 slices of bread from bag. Set them aside onto the plate.
- Open the jar of peanut butter. Dip the knife into the peanut butter.
- Now spread the peanut butter onto one slice of bread at a time with the desired amount of spread.
- After spreading the peanut butter onto the bread, put them together and slice the sandwich into 2 triangles
- 

## Done When
- The sandwich is covered with peanut butter on both slice of bread, put together and sliced in half on a plate.
